Story A:

Lu came in on a Monday after a three-day break. Well-earned too! She was on a rebound shift, and felt it. The Warden, Squee, called her in first thing and naturally, curious as to why and it wasn't always good to be singled out. Watchful eyes and such.

"Hey Lu, how was your break? Get some rest?" Squee asked.

"Yeah, not bad. I don't remember much of it. But I did. How were the three days here?"

"Same. I don't remember much of it." Squee said and pulled out paperwork from the top left drawer of the desk. Squee was a man of little words.

Squee invited Lu to sit by motioning to the chair across from the desk "I called you in because there's an opportunity, well suited for a guard in your training, work experience and skills. I'd like to offer it to you. You get time and a half, upgraded medical, full compensation for any out-of-pocket expenditures. It would be an unusual shift, but I think you'd be up to it."

"Thank you for considering me. What would the shift be, you said unusual." Squee replied "It's a night shift. You would get days off, so to speak. You no longer work a Monday to Friday grind."

What's the catch? Lu thought. "I feel I should tell you that you'd be assigned to a single "block". No rotation. Its a 5pm-5am time slot and you would be expected to stay over if needed. This "tenant" will be under special observation and may require an overnight here and there. There will be a special accommodation made for that as well. You wont be in the bunk." The bunk, as it was called, was a place for quick shut eye. It was a bunk bed set up with the basics and served the purpose.

"Special accommodation?" Lu said curiously. Squee looked at her and said " yes, you will have your own room for the nights, or rather days that you may need to stay."

"Can I ask, who is the "tenant"?

"We asked his name and he said his name was too long to pronounce or spell. Mumbled something and then came out with Dana."

"Carl?"

"Yes, Carl. Well, what do you think. Its time sensitive you give me the decision."

Lu sat and quickly reviewed the details. One inmate, one section, set schedule. She could do a night shift. Compensation sounds good. Got to pay the dues, right. Won't be forever.

"Sir, I'll do it. It would be a good for experience."

Squee pushed over the work contract and handed her the pen.

"Good to hear. I won't lie, it isnt going to be a cakewalk. I think you'll do good."

Lu signed and went to push the paperwork back. *Did her name just disappear...*

"Ok, go home. Get some supplies together. I'd suggest a second uniform. Extra things you can leave here. Toiletries, etc. Oh, do you have the extra pepper spray we handed out at Christmas? Bring that too. If not, I know there's extra in the supply room. I can't say it may not be needed, but better than nothing. No jewelry! Thats a must! Leave it at home."

"Your first shift is tonight. Be here at 4:30 pm. I'll walk you through it."

Lu left and did just what the warden said. Showered, grabbed her stuff, left all jewelry at home and got a quick nap in. Arriving early, she clocked in, headed straight for the warden's office. Was it her or was everyone now looking at her funny. She knocked. No answer. She waited and knocked again. She heard raised voices and peering through the partially broken blind that hung on the door, saw a guy that she swore was 8 feet tall and wearing...a cape?!

Before she could get back from the door it flew open and what she saw was exactly that! An 8-foot-tall man with...a cape and she was certain no feet were on the ground. Lu tried to look without looking, but no success. She could put money on it- he was floating.

"Come in, Lu." Squee slammed the bottom drawer shut, pushed his hand through his hair and tried to resemble the man she came to respect and admire and not look as shaken as he did three minutes ago.

"Great, you got everything and left the jewelry, right?" Lu nodded and held up the bag.

"Follow me." Squee and Lu walked the hall to where she thought was leading to the wing where the high profile or the more "dangerous" were kept. As they walked, he headed toward the door marked "EXIT". Walking down the hall to the original portion of the building, thinking it odd, Lu continued and refrained from small talk and just closely followed Squee. He abruptly turned right and was headed toward the out of service elevator.

She watched. He pulled out the keys. You know, the big "O" ring with every key ever made on it. He searched and found the one he needed. Quick look over the shoulder, he put the key in the lock next to the call button. Pressed down. Squee looked at her to get in.

"This is still working. I thought it was fully out of order."

"It still is operating. It was better that everyone thought it wasn't and I'm the only one with a key."

"Lu, there are a few things I need to tell you. First, I want to commend you for your blind bravery. Taking a post with not much detail on the detainee. I can't say many others would. I wanted to ask, you didn't ask me much regarding them and I wondered why?"

"Well, it's another post, right? Training prepares you to expect the unexpected and whether I know all or none about the persons under my watch, I'm to be ready."

Squee raised his eyebrows and did a little nod. Impressed. He felt even more assured he chose correctly.

"Lu, you're smart, clever, strong and observant. Don't feel your work has gone unnoticed."

"Thank you," Lu said.

They landed on the basement level. We stepped out. Lu was never in this part of the basement. She'd been down there before...thanks to a stupid, joke by Richards. She was still the newbie and thinking he'd be funny he sent me down to look for a file that did not exist, switched off the lights and had another guard jump out and tackle me. Lu broke the other guard's nose in the fight and Richards learned quickly to not mess with her. Let's say he couldn't stop scratching a very sensitive place and it all took place at mealtime. In front of *eveyone*. I mean everyone. The inmates, the guards, the security cameras. Nothing a little itching powder wouldn't fix. He was called "Jock itch" from there on out.

Mind over matter.

"Where are we?" Lu followed as he turned the ancient, nob and tube switch on the wall. The hallway lit up with a vein of bulbs. *Is this it? This is how I die?!* Lu thought.

"There was a sector of the old prison built over by the new. Only access is with this elevator. If everyone knew this was still here, lord knows what would go on down here." Squee walked ahead as if he knew the steps by heart.

"Before the prison was taken over and "added on to" if you will, to make room for more guests, this wasn't a normal prison. That was way before you time"

"How so..." Lu asked.

"We took the ones no other institution would take. The ones who did the worst and there was no place for them, so they came here. It wasn't a known location on any map. Imagine a whole other world happening under your feet. You come in, go to work, make sure everyone is safe, cared for and their time is checked off on a sheet of paper. But underneath is a separate existence. Well, that's starting up again. The lovely looking gentleman you saw earlier, he is the transporter, and we were settling the final arrangements. This isn't a one time, one inmate situation. The program is starting up again and I hoped it was over for good. The ungodly, the unnamed and the unknown were kept here. And we just received our newest occupant."

They arrived at what could only be described as the biggest, most secured cell she had ever seen. Easily a 5-foot by 5-foot cube of iron on iron.

Lu stood, eyes wide, bag tightening in her grip.

Across from them, in a still of the thickest darkness she had ever seen, a pair of glowing eyes opened. Lu was certain she wasn't breathing. She didn't remember breathing or even how to.

"Lu, have you ever heard of Dracula...."

Lu stood glued in her spot, this time her bag slipping out of her hand and hitting the floor.

"He's the job and he would like to be referred to as "Carl". Squee said but Lu could only hear her heart beating in her ears.

Slowly, the figure moved, and those eyes never moved off of her. Drcaula or "Carl" came to the entrance of the cell. Stood and Squee grabbed Lu's wrist.

"Always stay on the line..." Lu heard Squee say in a low tone. Looking down she saw the metal bar in the stone ground.

Lu stood.

A long-fingered hand stretched out in greeting.

"Good Evening, Squee..."

"Good Evening, Carl..."
