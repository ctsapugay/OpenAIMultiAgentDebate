Story B:

She thought she could carry on this charade for longer. After all, why would the mighty Vixen regret hurting someone? She'd done far worse, many times over, so why should this bother her? It was true that she had come to be quite fond of Kevin. Some might call it love. She'd often laughed at the thought, Vixen being in love. No, Vixen was incapable of love.

But Jessica? Perhaps she was not only capable of loving someone, but had fallen in love with this fool named Kevin. Kevin, with his ridiculously sweet poems, his absurdly long-winded but well-meaning love letters. His seeming inability to ignore a stranger in need. He was quite charming really, if a bit boring. Vanilla. Just plain *good*. Which is why Vixen's tryst with Lieutenant Altruism had to end. Jessica could no longer take the stress of betraying Kevin's trust.

It had all started on a whim. Vixen and Lt. Altruism had been battling for about as long as either of them had been around. He had just sort of popped up during one of her early heists. Her plans had grown in scope since then, but somehow, so had his ability to thwart her, most of the time anyways.

So she had decided to seduce him. It was just a game to her. Give him a taste of the dark side so to speak. It had almost been too easy. She had pegged a goody-two-shoes like him as someone who'd have settled down with some old maid, but apparently not, because he was all too eager to play with her. It gave her a chance to really cut loose and express her inner desires in a way Jessica, or Kevin for that matter, couldn't handle.

And there was something a little exciting about not knowing who was under the mask. Not that it mattered. She was pretty sure Lt. Altruism had an 'alter ego', but the mask was his real face. Some criminals might have even feared it, but she honestly just thought it looked a little goofy. Almost like one of those wacky-waving-inflatable-arm-waving-tube-men. After it had rained.

So she had had gone downtown to rob a bank. Something that would grab attention but not generally threaten the general populace the way some of her other plots did. This had become her signal to let him know she was in the mood for some 'sparring'.

It took him less time than usual to get there. All the old ladies were already across the street she supposed.

"Vixen! Let these people go!" He cried when he arrived. He always was one for theatrics. She supposed she would ham it up a bit too. She did enjoy giving him a hard time.

"Lieutenant," she said with a mock salute, "you got here quick. What, no kittens in trees that needed to be rescued?"

"Please Vixen, let's not do this today. Let the people go and we can settle this elsewhere."

She frowned. He was usually better at playing ball than this. Something must have had him off his game.

"Fine, fine. The people can go, but I'm taking the money this poor teller collected for me. Wouldn't want all his hard work to go to waste." She winked at the teller and smiled as he nervously shook his head.

She grabbed the bag of money and took off, heading towards the secluded spot in the mountains where they usually met. Lt. Altruism arrived not long after she did. There was no easy way to do something like this, so best to just rip off the Band-aid.

"We need to talk."

"We need to talk."

He looked so uncomfortable, she almost felt bad for the guy.

"Go ahead," he said.

"Please, by all means, ladies first."

He gave her a look which said that he wanted to say something at that, but thought better of it. Something must've really been eating away at him. This ought to be interesting.

"I can't do this anymore."

Well that's convenient. She wouldn't even have to be the bad guy in this break-up. But she could still make him squirm a little.

"What do you mean? You think we should fight other people?" she smirked.

"You know what I mean. This. Us."

"Whoa there big guy, you're moving a little fast for me, you know I'm not one to settle down."

She smiled inwardly at the lie.

"Fine, whatever. You know what I mean. I just. I can't keep doing this. I can't hurt her anymore."

Vixen's eyebrows shot up at that. Could he really be that fallible, that, human? This could be very interesting.

"Her?"

"I never should have betrayed her, she means so much to me. She might push my buttons sometimes, but I love her and she doesn't deserve this."

"Lieutenant, don't tell me. You couldn't possibly have a girlfriend?"

She was practically cackling with glee. To think her plan to seduce him would have taken this much of a toll. He looked on the verge of tears.

"No. She's my...my wife."

Vixen let the appropriate amount of shock display on her face, then twisted it into a mocking sneer.

"Surely not, lieutenant, you would never do such a thing!"

The pain in his eyes was apparent. This had clearly been causing him even more turmoil than her own situation had her.

"Jess, she doesn't know... she doesn't know what I am. I don't think she'd understand."

Vixen's shock was no longer feigned. Of course people called her Jess, but no one quite like Kevin. Except for Lieutenant Altruism apparently. Suddenly it was clear as day to her. How could she have been so blind?

Well, she'd have to spend some time thinking about that later, but now she found herself in a rather amusing predicament. She actually laughed out loud.

"All that misplaced guilt," she said. "To think I was worried over nothing."

Kevin's face betrayed his bemusement. He said nothing.

"Kevin, Kevin, Kevin, would you look at the two of us. And here all this time I thought you were such a straight-laced goody-goody. It's nice to see you actually know how to live a little."

"What are you talking about? How do you know my name?"

Kevin always was a bit slow on the uptake.

"You don't have to worry about Jess, because she doesn't mind that you were 'cheating' on her," she used air quotes for emphasis, "in fact, she finds the whole thing rather funny."

"What are you..."

She removed her mask.

"It's me silly, ta da! Oh this is too much." She could not stop herself from laughing and the look of utter bewilderment on Kevin's face did not help the matter.

"Jess. I had no idea. I... Why would you let this go on for so long? As a joke?"

"Oh, I had no idea until just now either! That's what makes it so funny. And I mean, really I was just having a bit of a laugh at the 'lieutenant's' expense, but to think all along I was still just sleeping with you. You know, this could really spice up the bedroom, I can hardly believe how different you are with the mask on, maybe we could even-"

"Jess." His voice was hard and cold. "What do you mean you didn't know?"

"Well, clearly it wouldn't have been as fun to corrupt Lt. Altruism if I had known it would be at my own expense. I mean to be honest, I wasn't even sure you had someone, even though I was sure you must have and-"

"So you cheated on me?"

"Well, no, not actually. That's the funny thing, we were always only sleeping with each other."

"But you didn't know. You wanted to do it. You didn't even recognize me."

"Right, but I could say the same thing. And aren't you supposed to be the good-"

"Stop."

"-guy? Look at it this way, I've preserved your morals, your code, your-"

"STOP!" He roared. "Just shut up! How could you do this to me?! It's not enough to betray me and sleep with another man-"

"Well, technically-"

"-but you also convince me to betray my own moral compass, my very being, who I am. What I stand for."

"You didn't make it hard, I mean just think back-"

"I can't forgive you for this. You've gone too far. There is no punishment great enough for you to atone for the sins you have committed. Nothing I can do will make you hurt the way you have hurt me." His face which had been contorted with fury suddenly became calm. "But I will do what I can."

He advanced towards her.

"Kevin, please, I think you're overreacting."

"Not Kevin," he said.

"Fine, Lieutenant Altruism, just-"

"It's Hammurabi now."
